# Permission justifications (Principle 24 — one sentence a frightened user accepts, each)

| Permission       | Why                                                                              |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| tabs             | See and keep your tabs' titles and addresses so they can be restored.            |
| tabGroups        | Keep your group structure (names, colors, order) when parking and resuming.      |
| sessions         | Recover your windows and tabs after Chrome closes unexpectedly.                  |
| storage          | Your archive lives on your device — this is where it is kept.                    |
| unlimitedStorage | So your archive never runs out of space.                                         |
| alarms           | Maintenance on schedule (backups, cleanups you configured).                      |
| offscreen        | Heavy work (AI naming/summaries, imports/exports) without slowing your browsing. |
| favicon          | Show site icons in your library.                                                 |
| contextMenus     | Park tabs from right-click.                                                      |

## Never requested at install (law: ADR-022 / S-06)

notifications · downloads · cookies · history · host permissions · scripting.
Optional (runtime, consent-sheet first, per §6.8): per-site content indexing — not requested in v1.
